Day 1 - Question 1: Customer Order Analysis
Difficulty: Easy-Medium
Topics: JOIN, WHERE, GROUP BY, HAVING, COUNT, SUM, ORDER BY

Question
--------
You have two tables:

Table 1: customers

+-------------+-------+
| customer_id | name  |
+-------------+-------+
| 1           | Asha  |
| 2           | Kiran |
| 3           | Meera |
| 4           | Rahul |
| 5           | Diya  |
+-------------+-------+

Table 2: orders

+----------+-------------+--------+-----------+
| order_id | customer_id | amount | status    |
+----------+-------------+--------+-----------+
| 101      | 1           | 1200   | Delivered |
| 102      | 1           | 900    | Cancelled |
| 103      | 1           | 1600   | Delivered |
| 104      | 2           | 900    | Delivered |
| 105      | 2           | 700    | Delivered |
| 106      | 3           | 2500   | Delivered |
| 107      | 4           | 600    | Delivered |
| 108      | 4           | 500    | Delivered |
| 109      | 5           | 400    | Cancelled |
+----------+-------------+--------+-----------+

Task:
Find customers who have at least 2 delivered orders, considering only
orders where amount >= 500.

Display:
- customer_name
- delivered_orders
- total_amount

Sort by highest total_amount first.

Expected Output
---------------
+---------------+------------------+--------------+
| customer_name | delivered_orders | total_amount |
+---------------+------------------+--------------+
| Asha          | 2                | 2800         |
| Kiran         | 2                | 1600         |
| Rahul         | 2                | 1100         |
+---------------+------------------+--------------+

My SQL Query
------------
SELECT c.name as customer_name, COUNT(*) AS delivered_orders, SUM(o.amount) AS total_amount
FROM orders o
LEFT JOIN customers c ON o.customer_id = c.customer_id
WHERE o.status = 'Delivered' AND o.amount >= 500
GROUP BY c.name, c.customer_id
HAVING COUNT(*) >= 2
ORDER BY SUM(o.amount) DESC

Day 1 - Question 2: Employee Salary Update
Difficulty: Easy-Medium
Topics: UPDATE, SET, CASE, WHERE

Question
--------
You have the following employees table:

+--------+-------+------------+--------+------------+
| emp_id | name  | department | salary | experience |
+--------+-------+------------+--------+------------+
| 1      | Rahul | Backend    | 50000  | 2          |
| 2      | Priya | Backend    | 80000  | 5          |
| 3      | Amit  | QA         | 40000  | 1          |
| 4      | Sneha | Backend    | 60000  | 3          |
| 5      | Karan | QA         | 70000  | 4          |
| 6      | Riya  | DevOps     | 90000  | 6          |
+--------+-------+------------+--------+------------+

Task:
Write one SQL UPDATE statement that:
1. Increases salary by 20% for Backend employees having 3 or more years of experience.
2. Increases salary by 10% for Backend employees having less than 3 years of experience.
3. Does not modify employees from other departments.
4. Performs the entire update using one SQL statement.

Expected Table After Update
---------------------------
+-------+------------+--------+
| name  | department | salary |
+-------+------------+--------+
| Rahul | Backend    | 55000  |
| Priya | Backend    | 96000  |
| Amit  | QA         | 40000  |
| Sneha | Backend    | 72000  |
| Karan | QA         | 70000  |
| Riya  | DevOps     | 90000  |
+-------+------------+--------+

My SQL Query
------------
UPDATE employees 
SET salary = CASE 
       WHEN experience >= 3 THEN salary + (salary * 0.2)
       ELSE salary + (salary * 0.1)
END
WHERE department = 'Backend'

Day 2 - Question 1: Warehouse Restock Queue
Difficulty: Easy-Medium
Topics: SELECT, WHERE, Computed Columns, ORDER BY

Question
--------
The products table contains current stock and the minimum reorder level for each product. Only active products should appear in the restock queue.

Table: products

+------------+--------------+-------------+-------+---------------+-----------+
| product_id | product_name | category    | stock | reorder_level | is_active |
+------------+--------------+-------------+-------+---------------+-----------+
| 1          | Keyboard     | Accessories | 5     | 10            | 1         |
| 2          | Mouse        | Accessories | 12    | 10            | 1         |
| 3          | Monitor      | Electronics | 2     | 5             | 1         |
| 4          | Webcam       | Electronics | 0     | 8             | 1         |
| 5          | USB Hub      | Accessories | 3     | 5             | 0         |
| 6          | Laptop       | Electronics | 14    | 10            | 1         |
| 7          | Headphones   | Electronics | 4     | 4             | 1         |
| 8          | Charger      | Accessories | 1     | 5             | 1         |
+------------+--------------+-------------+-------+---------------+-----------+

Task:
1. Write a SELECT query to list products that need restocking.
2. Include only active products (is_active = 1) whose stock is strictly below reorder_level.
3. Display product_name, stock, and a computed column named shortage, equal to reorder_level - stock.
4. Order by shortage from highest to lowest. When shortages tie, order by product_name alphabetically.

Expected Output
---------------
+--------------+-------+----------+
| product_name | stock | shortage |
+--------------+-------+----------+
| Webcam       | 0     | 8        |
| Keyboard     | 5     | 5        |
| Charger      | 1     | 4        |
| Monitor      | 2     | 3        |
+--------------+-------+----------+

My SQL Query
------------
SELECT product_name, stock, (reorder_level - stock) AS shortage
FROM products
WHERE is_active = 1 AND stock < reorder_level
ORDER BY shortage DESC, product_name ASC
